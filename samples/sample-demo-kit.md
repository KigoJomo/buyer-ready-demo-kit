# Sample Demo Kit

## Scenario

A small logistics software vendor is pitching a delivery tracking portal to an SME distributor.

## Buyer pain

The distributor manages delivery status through calls and spreadsheets. Sales staff cannot confidently answer customer questions, and managers only see delays after complaints arrive.

## Demo storyline

1. Capture a new delivery request.
2. Assign it to a driver or dispatch owner.
3. Update delivery status from pending to in transit to delivered.
4. Show the customer service dashboard.
5. Export a weekly delay report for management.

## Discovery questions

- How many delivery status calls does the team handle per day?
- Which delay types are most common?
- Who needs visibility: sales, dispatch, finance, or management?
- What report does leadership ask for most often?

## Objection handling

Objection: We already use spreadsheets.

Response: Spreadsheets may still work for recording data, but they are weak for live status, ownership, and alerts. The pilot can focus only on visibility and escalation before touching the rest of the process.

## Proposal one-pager summary

Build a lightweight delivery status portal for one dispatch team, with request capture, status updates, and a weekly delay report. Success is measured by fewer status calls and faster escalation of delayed deliveries.
