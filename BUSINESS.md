# Business Plan

## Business model

Productized presales writing and demo preparation for B2B software teams.

## Why this can start with zero money

- Delivery is manual and skill-based.
- GitHub provides a public repo, issue intake, and static files.
- The free toolkit acts as a lead magnet.
- Outreach can happen through LinkedIn, X, GitHub, email, and existing network.
- Payment can be arranged only after a buyer agrees.

## Initial offer

Buyer-Ready Demo Sprint: USD 75 or KES equivalent.

## Ideal customer profile

- Early SaaS/product founder
- IT consultancy selling software implementation
- Agency pitching a web app, portal, CRM, ERP, automation, or reporting project
- Team has a product/service but weak sales material

## Fulfillment checklist

1. Review intake.
2. Confirm fit and payment.
3. Ask for missing product, buyer, pricing, and demo context.
4. Draft discovery bank.
5. Draft demo storyline.
6. Draft objection sheet.
7. Draft one-page proposal.
8. Draft follow-up emails.
9. Send editable files.

## First 10 lead targets

- Local SaaS founders
- Nairobi web/software agencies
- ERP/CRM implementers
- Startup founders posting product launches
- GitHub projects with business-facing SaaS demos

## Success metric

First sale or three qualified calls within 14 days of launch.
