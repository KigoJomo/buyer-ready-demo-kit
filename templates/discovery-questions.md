# Discovery Questions

## Buyer context

- What triggered the search for a solution now?
- Who owns the problem today?
- What happens if the problem remains unsolved for another quarter?
- Which team feels the pain most often?

## Current process

- What tools or workarounds are being used today?
- Where does the process slow down or break?
- Which manual steps create the most risk?
- What data is hard to find, trust, or act on?

## Value and urgency

- What metric would improve if this worked well?
- How is that metric reviewed internally?
- What business outcome would make this project worth funding?
- Is there a deadline, contract, audit, launch, or leadership priority driving this?

## Decision process

- Who signs off technically?
- Who signs off commercially?
- What concerns would block a decision?
- What proof would make the team comfortable moving forward?
