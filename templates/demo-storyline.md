# Demo Storyline

## 1. Buyer pain

Open with the buyer's current situation in plain language.

Example:
The operations team is losing time because requests arrive across email, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets. Managers cannot see status until someone manually updates a tracker.

## 2. Business cost

Connect the pain to a measurable business risk.

Example:
This creates delayed approvals, missed follow-ups, and weak reporting when leadership asks for pipeline or service status.

## 3. Desired future

Describe what the buyer wants to be true after implementation.

Example:
Every request is captured once, routed to the right owner, and visible on a live dashboard.

## 4. Demo path

- Show how a new request is captured
- Show how the right person gets assigned
- Show status visibility and notifications
- Show reporting or export for management
- Close with implementation steps

## 5. Proof points

- Time saved
- Errors reduced
- Visibility improved
- Adoption made easier

## 6. Closing question

Based on what we walked through, where would this create the most immediate value for your team?
